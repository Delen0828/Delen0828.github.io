Research Projects

  • Reverse-Engineering Insight Formation from Visualizations using Brain and Eye Signals
    Advisors: Cindy Xiong Bearfield (Georgia Tech), Ratan Murty (Georgia Tech)
    • Investigating how low-level perceptual features interact with high-level reasoning processes in visualization comprehension.
    • Combining fMRI and eye-tracking to quantify perceptual and cognitive load across visual cortex and frontal cortex.
    • Developing a computational model to predict and interpret perceptual-cognitive demands from visualizations.
  • VisChatter – Visual Annotations for Collaborative Analytics with Multimodality
    Advisors: Cindy Xiong Bearfield (Georgia Tech), Tong Yu (Adobe)
    • Designed and implemented an interactive visualization dashboard with real-time annotation for synchronous collaborative analytics.
    • Integrated speech recognition, LLM-based keyword extraction, and custom annotation APIs for seamless multimodal input.
    • Conducted controlled A/B testing, measuring user engagement and task efficiency improvements.
  • Interactive Web Integration for Gramm Visualizations in MATLAB
    Advisors: Cindy Xiong Bearfield (Georgia Tech), Vijay Iyer (MathWorks), Pierre Morel (ULCO)
    • Extended Gramm, an open-source MATLAB visualization library, with a feature to export plots as interactive web visualizations.
    • Integrated Vega and SVG-based interactivity to enable zooming, panning, and tooltips directly in the browser.
    • Collaborated with the open-source community to design, implement, and document the new functionality.
  • Assessing Immersiveness of Visual Storytelling in VR with Bio-signals
    Advisors: Jeffery Riedmiller (Dolby Research), Cindy Xiong Bearfield (Georgia Tech)
    • Developed a pipeline to collect and analyze physiological signals during VR storytelling.
    • Piloting VR scenes varying in narrative structure and visual intensity.
    • Conducting data-driven evaluation of perceived immersion and emotional response.
  • Vision Rehabilitation through Multisensory Interaction
    Advisors: Tina Liu (Georgetown University), Cindy Xiong Bearfield (Georgia Tech)
    • Built interactive prototypes for training spatial attention and visual reconstruction.
    • Integrated visual and auditory cues to enhance sensory compensation and engagement.
    • Piloting test sessions with clinical collaborators to assess user feedback and accessibility.
  • Assessing Cognitive Load of Word-scale Visualizations
    Advisors: Marti Hearst (UC Berkeley), Cindy Xiong Bearfield (Georgia Tech)
    • Designed and executed a controlled eye-tracking study to assess how embedded visual embellishments affect reading comprehension across varied data densities.
    • Quantified cognitive disruption by analyzing psychophysiological metrics (eye saccades).
    • Explored word-scale visualization design guidelines for fluent reading flow.
