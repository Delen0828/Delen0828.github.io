I'm a fourth-year CS Ph.D. student at Georgia Tech, advised by <a href='https://cyxiong.com/'>Dr. Cindy Xiong Bearfield</a>. Prior to joining Georgia Tech, I worked with <a href='https://shuaili8.github.io/'>Dr. Shuai Li</a> at Shanghai Jiao Tong University.

My research interest focuses on **understanding reasoning process on data visualization by modeling behavior, physiological, and neuro signal**, spanning from **visualization animation and interaction**, **collaborative visualization systems**, to **visualization recommender systems**. I explore how design choices impact perception in animated and interactive visualization, how AI assistants can enhance data collaboration with visualization systems, and how to recommend visualizations with minimal user interaction.

I also have experience with *general recommender systems*, *deep learning*, *gesture recognition* and *reinforcement learning*.
